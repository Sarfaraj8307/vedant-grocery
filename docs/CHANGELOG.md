## 2026-10-07 · mobile QA follow-up (this batch)

- **Hero — double-composition fixed.** `HeroComposition` now splits the drawing into `background` (wash + shadow, always visible) and `subject` (basket/produce/leaves/ticks). `HeroVisual` mounts lazily, latches `mounted` (never tears the canvas down on scroll — stops WebGL context churn), and fades the subject out on the **first painted frame** (`PaintedSignal` via `useFrame`) instead of on \"context created\" or a timeout. `SceneBoundary` now accepts `onFailure` so a late GPU/context loss restores the subject (no empty hero).
- **Catalogue cards — 360px ADD clip fixed.** At 360px a card's content box is ~144px and `₹104  ₹130  [ADD]` needs ~165px; the stepper's `min-width` (needed so ADD↔stepper don't reflow) pushed it 11px past the card's `overflow-hidden` edge. `ProductCard`/`ProductRailCard` now use `priceRow: flex items-end justify-between; PriceTag: flex-wrap; stepper: shrink-0` so the struck-through compare price wraps to its own line rather than compressing or clipping the control.
- **Section headings — single-word orphan fixed.** `SectionHeading` (`/`, `/categories` etc.) was `flex items-end justify-between`; at 360px the title shrank to ~160px → \"Everything / we / have today\". Now `flex flex-wrap items-end justify-between gap-y-2` — the action (`Search instead`, `Browse all`) moves to its own line instead of squeezing the heading.
- **Sort label — mis-centred when filters wrapped.** `SortControl` was `flex items-center`; with wrapped chips the \"Sort\" caption floated between two chip rows. Now `items-start` with `leading-8` (= chip height) so it sits on the first row.
- Verified: `pnpm build` ✓, `tsc --noEmit` ✓, browser evaluate at 360/392 (hero subject opacity 0 after paint; section heading no overflow; card price row expected to wrap on narrow cards). Screenshots at 360/392/1280 confirm visible hero, correct price row, and no doc-level horizontal overflow (`docScrollW == clientW`).
